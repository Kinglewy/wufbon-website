Program 1 · Civic power engine

Temba Emeeza (“Climb the Table”) One Power Movement

Civic rights, worker power and a just energy transition. When communities organize, understand their rights and act collectively, they become unstoppable.

How it works

From isolated grievances to collective action

Structured as WUFBON's autonomous, non-partisan civic and economic power engine, Temba Emeeza transforms isolated grievances across the Albertine Graben into structured, evidence-based collective action.

Drawing on 800 religious leader subscribers and 146 Faith-Based Organisations, we systematically organize tea plantation workers, agricultural outgrowers, teachers, artisanal small-scale miners and rural youth to defend their land, labor and ecological rights.

Four operational quadrants

Organizing scope & self-reliance engines

Operational quadrantCore grassroots organizing & advocacy scopeSelf-reliance & sustainability engine

Worker Power & Inter-Labor Union OrganizingOrganizing tea plantation workers, agricultural outgrowers, teachers and artisanal miners (Butunduzi); monthly Community Barazas; inter-labor mediation tables; binding Collective Bargaining Agreements (CBAs); eradicating child labor and toxic mercury use.Union member dues & institutionalized employer-worker mediation councils

Youth Movement Building & Interfaith Media NetworkEquipping regional youth chapters across 10 district hubs; coordinating 25 radio stations and digital channels (100,000+ monthly listeners); digitized Manifesto Implementation Matrix; voter rights literacy and the Alternatives to Violence Project (AVP).Commercial advertising slots on the 25-station Interfaith Media Network
3. Just Energy Transition & Clean-Energy Micro-FranchisesScaling 60+ village-led retail micro-franchises; training interfaith youth chapters, women's VSLAs and PWDs as certified entrepreneurs selling tier-1 solar home systems and clean cookstoves to replace charcoal and kerosene.Revolving wholesale-to-retail clean-energy enterprise margins
4. Extractive Sector Accountability & Oil/Gas WatchdogsTraining grassroots watchdog teams across Kyenjojo, Kyegegwa and the Albertine Graben to log environmental and social impacts of oil, gas and mining infrastructure, enforce fair land compensation and audit public revenues.Integrated into district Community Barazas & citizen audit committees
