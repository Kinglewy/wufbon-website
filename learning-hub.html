Inside this issue

What you will learn

Welcoming the Stranger, Geneva

With support from Faith to Action Network and the Lutheran World Federation, WUFBON joined the “Welcoming the Stranger, Shaping the Future” conference on how faith communities can welcome and integrate refugees, migrants and internally displaced people.

Minorities for minorities: Solidarity Pride Month

Engagement with minorities in Kyegegwa and Kamwenge on Freedom of Religion or Belief, SRHR and family planning. A refugee solidarity group drafted an action plan and by-laws and is pushing for registration so it can advocate directly.

What will it take to stop climate change?

Working with Kogere Foundation Uganda and our Ekyooto (camp fire) dialogues, faith and cultural actors along the Muzizi river in Kyenjojo, “the land of elephants”, formed an inter-agency initiative for climate action.

Clean energy and the strategic plan

WUFBON's commitment to renewable energy and distributed energy solutions, and the strategic plan's focus areas: human rights and governance; health, education, sports and culture; natural resources and clean energy; community services; and research.
