Donate & partner

Co-invest in lasting self-reliance

Invest in community-owned assets, not endless charity loops. Whether you are a foundation, bilateral agency, faith-based philanthropy, corporate social investor or individual supporter, your contribution is catalytic seed capital.

Five strategic investment tracks

Choose where your capital works

Track 1

Temba Emeeza Civic & Labor Power

What your partnership capitalizes

Worker union organizing, monthly Community Barazas, 25-station Interfaith Media Network broadcasts, AVP peacebuilding and extractive sector watchdogs.

Permanent self-reliance multiplier

Sustained by union dues & commercial media advertising slots.

Track 2

Women & Children's Clinical Care

What your partnership capitalizes

Maternal, neonatal, fistula, cervical cancer, HIV/SRHR, mental health and 4WD rugged ambulance outreaches at Caren McNelly McCormack Health Facility. Donate to the hospital →

Permanent self-reliance multiplier

Private clinical wing revenues cross-subsidize free care for vulnerable mothers & children.

Track 3

Interfaith Green Schools & OVC Meals

What your partnership capitalizes

Image of God Kindergarten & 52+ Interfaith Green Schools: tree nurseries, rainwater harvesting, clean kitchens and daily bio-intensive school meals for OVCs.

Permanent self-reliance multiplier

School agroforestry gardens & district education integration sustain daily feeding.

Track 4

Clean Energy & Pink Economy Capital

What your partnership capitalizes

Revolving seed loans via Quaker Financial Services for PWDs & marginalized entrepreneurs, plus 60+ youth/women clean-energy retail micro-franchises.

Permanent self-reliance multiplier

Revolving loan repayments & wholesale-to-retail solar/cookstove margins.

Track 5

Nsinde Eco-Beach & Wildlife Defense

What your partnership capitalizes

Muzizi–Lake Albert water-quality lab, indigenous riverbank reforestation, anti-poaching watchdogs and elephant buffer fencing across Kyenjojo's forest reserves.

Permanent self-reliance multiplier

100% of Nsinde Eco-Beach hospitality profits ring-fenced for conservation.
