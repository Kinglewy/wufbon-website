Section I · Flagship infrastructure

Caren McNelly McCormack Women & Children Health Facility

Rural Health Centre IV Complex · Kigoba (Nsinde), Kyenjojo District · Formally renamed November 2026

“This facility is located in a village, behind a village, within a village—built right where mothers and children need it most.”

Rev. Atuhaire Moses, Founder & Executive Director, WUFBON

From brick walls raised with community and founder support to a completed Health Centre IV.

A registered Rural Health Centre IV with a full outpatient department, sterile maternity suite and 25-bed inpatient wards, staffed 24/7 and governed by a 9-member Committee of Village Elders.

2023–2026

The FAITH + Type 1 Diabetes Care NetworkActive

Partner: Lions Club of Kyenjojo · 10 rural health nodes, Kyenjojo District+

Decentralized hub-and-spoke design

In rural Western Uganda, pediatric Type 1 Diabetes has carried severe morbidity because insulin stocks sat at distant regional referral hospitals, rural Health Centre IIs and IIIs lacked cold-chain storage, and glucometers and strips were unaffordable. Launched in 2023 by WUFBON and the Lions Club of Kyenjojo, the network decentralized pediatric diabetes management across 10 rural health nodes, with uninterrupted supply lines for insulin, syringes, calibrated glucometers and testing strips within walking or short transit distance of farming households.

Home cold-chain storage, family literacy & emergency protocols

Parents, caregivers, school teachers and local clergy were trained together to recognize early symptoms of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) and severe hypoglycemia. For off-grid households without refrigeration, nurses and Lions Club volunteers introduced validated evaporative cooling clay-pot storage, temperature logs and rapid clinic-nurse emergency referral lines.

2025–2026

Quaker Financial Services: The Pink Fund for Queer & Marginalized GroupsActive

Partner: Stone Run Friends Meeting (grant received 2025) · Western Uganda+

Inclusive microfinance & economic dignity

WUFBON established The Pink Fund to address economic exclusion, lending discrimination and livelihood insecurity faced by queer and marginalized community members in rural Western Uganda, providing stigma-free, zero-collateral revolving micro-loans, savings mechanisms and financial literacy mentorship.

Peer-governed livelihood resilience

Confidential, peer-led savings circles enable participants to launch tailoring, agriculture, retail and artisan micro-enterprises, while fostering mutual aid and psychosocial solidarity outside discriminatory financial structures.

2021–2025

Joint Initiative for Strategic Religious Action (JISRA)

Partners: Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Faith to Action Network, Mensen met een Missie, Search for Common Ground, Tearfund & NRTP · Official JISRA Certificate of Completion+

Global consortium standing, reach & certification

Recognized on jisra.org as one of 50 civil society partners across seven countries (Ethiopia, Indonesia, Iraq, Kenya, Mali, Nigeria and Uganda) and awarded an Official JISRA Certificate of Completion, WUFBON implemented this five-year Freedom of Religion and Belief (FoRB) program. It reached 250,000+ citizens and mobilized 150+ religious leaders, women of faith and youth influencers: 86 women and girls (57%), 54 men and boys (36%) and 10 marginalized and PWD representatives (7%).

Radical inclusion

Persons with albinism, queer individuals and minority religious actors gained direct access to and voice within sub-national and national decision-making forums. Scripture-grounded dialogues challenged gender-based violence and child marriage. Cross-denominational peace committees united Catholic, Anglican, Pentecostal, Muslim, Adventist and minority faith leaders to mediate land and school disputes, and the WUFBON District Youth Council presented its JISRA policy report to State Minister Hon. Nyirabashitsi Sarah Mateke, MPs, Tooro cultural leaders and cultural musician Kigambo Araali.

Digital empowerment & grassroots grant-making

WUFBON equipped 10 District Interfaith Hubs with office equipment and digital tools, including computer donations to the Bundibugyo Association of Albinos, Bundibugyo Association of Religious Leaders, Kabarole Interfaith and Inter-Cultural Hub and Kamwenge District Interfaith Hub. Through JISRA's Participatory Grant-Making model, WUFBON awarded sub-grants to Ekitebe Ekikuru, Tradition, Environment and Peace Actors Alliance, and the Interfaith Youth Brass Band.

2020–2024

Accelerating Family Planning, Climate Resilience & COVID-19 Response

Partners: 128 Collective / Preston-Werner Ventures & Panorama Global · Kyenjojo District+

Rapid COVID-19 response & interfaith risk communication

WUFBON partnered with Kyenjojo District Local Government to design and operationalize the district's COVID-19 Response, Preparedness & Recovery Rapid Plan, mobilizing clergy, radio and community leaders to deliver accurate public health guidance.

Family planning & climate awareness through poetry and youth VSLAs (33,000 reached)

An integrated climate-SRHR curriculum paired with poetry, community theater, music and radio edutainment reached 33,000 participants. WUFBON formed 50 youth VSLAs, enabling 450 young women to access micro-loans and modern contraceptives, and secured district commitments integrating SRHR and climate mitigation into the District Nutrition Action Plan and 2023–2027 contraceptive financing.

2023–2024

Climate Change Awareness through Poetry, Lyrics, Music & Sports

Partner: KOSHE (Korea Safety Health Environment Foundation) · Rwenzori river basins+

Kinetic & musical climate mobilization

Inter-district Christian and Muslim football and netball tournaments featured half-time peer dialogues on watershed degradation, agroforestry and wetland conservation.

Local-language poetry & riverbank planting

Youth artists composed lyrics, music and spoken-word poetry in Rutooro/Runyoro translating climate science into local proverbs and songs. Every event ended with players, musicians, clergy and leaders planting indigenous species such as Markhamia lutea and Prunus africana on degraded riverbanks, with youth clubs monitoring sapling survival.

2020–2023

HIV/AIDS Structural Advocacy for Key Populations

Partner: Frontline AIDS (United Kingdom) · Kyenjojo & Western Uganda faith health networks+

Dismantling congregational stigma

Using religion-specific theological briefs co-developed with clerics and clinicians, WUFBON trained pastors, priests and imams to replace stigmatizing rhetoric with compassionate, rights-based messaging, turning churches and mosques into stigma-free referral spaces.

Peer navigators across the HIV care cascade

Trained community peer navigators worked alongside Village Health Teams across the UNAIDS 95-95-95 continuum: distributing HIV self-testing kits, linking newly diagnosed people to friendly clinics, facilitating discreet ART refills and hosting adherence clubs.

2020–2022

The Adolescent Reproductive Health Policy Project

Partner: Faith for Family Health Initiative (3FHI) · Kyenjojo District health facilities+

Localizing youth-inclusive clinical guidelines

During pandemic school closures that drove up adolescent pregnancies, WUFBON and 3FHI convened District Health Officers, facility managers, parents and faith leaders to co-customize youth-inclusive district clinical guidelines.

Adolescent-friendly health corners

Dedicated youth-branded consultation rooms inside rural clinics, protected after-school hours (3:30 PM – 6:00 PM) and Saturday clinics staffed by sensitized providers.

2019–2022

“Youth Are Able” Civic, SRHR & Economic Empowerment Project

Partners: FHI 360 / USAID Advancing Partners & Communities (APC) · Kyegegwa District+

Mobile Youth Truck outreach (47,100 youth reached)

WUFBON's audio-visual Mobile Youth Truck visited rural markets, trading centers and faith gatherings to reach 47,100 youth: 24,492 girls (52%), 19,311 boys (41%) and 3,297 marginalized and PWD youth (7%), with SRHR education, civic literacy and clinical referrals.

Clinical capacity, contraceptive uptake & youth enterprises

20 rural medical workers trained in youth-friendly FP counseling generated 3,000+ adolescent new FP users (1,560 girls, 1,230 boys, 210 others). 2,500+ out-of-school youth (1,300 girls, 1,025 boys, 175 others) were organized into VSLAs and vocational enterprises.

2018–2021

Health & Family Planning Public Expenditure Tracking

Partner: Faith for Family Health Initiative (3FHI) · Kyenjojo District rural health centres+

Kyenjojo's first faith-led expenditure count

Trained clergy, congregational monitors and Health Unit Management Committee members audited district budget releases and National Medical Stores delivery vouchers against stock cards and pharmacy shelves in Kyenjojo's first faith-led Public Expenditure Tracking Survey (PETS).

Public medicine disclosure boards

After every NMS delivery cycle, clinic in-charges and faith monitors publicly posted quantities of essential drugs and FP commodities received and expended, deterring diversion and building trust.

2018–2021

Communities Richer in Diversity (CRID) Peace Initiative

Partners: Faith to Action Network, European Union, ACRL-RfP & AACC · 8 Rwenzori districts+

Regional pluralism & youth peacebuilding (60,000+ youth)

Reached 60,000+ youth (31,200 girls, 24,600 boys, 4,200 marginalized and PWD youth) through Regional Interfaith Peace Caravans, student-led school diversity clubs and rapid-response interfaith mediation teams.

2017–2020

Faith + Family Planning Advocacy & Policy Accountability

Partners: Population Action International (PAI) & Faith to Action Network · Kyenjojo & Kagadi+

Theological consensus & SMART budget advocacy

Catholic, Anglican, Pentecostal and Muslim leaders built interfaith consensus on healthy birth spacing and used SMART budget advocacy to secure the Kyenjojo District FP Costed Implementation Plan (2017–2022) and a ring-fenced UGX 10,000,000 annual budget line for FP commodities and outreach.

2013–2016

Institutional & Organizational Capacity Enhancement

Partner: Korea National Council of Consumer Organizations (KNCF) · WUFBON Secretariat & member CBOs+

Fiduciary integrity, audit trails & PWD revolving funds

WUFBON established double-entry accounting, segregated procurement controls, independent audit readiness, disaggregated M&E and an inclusive interfaith governance charter, and launched peer-governed revolving livelihood funds for PWDs in tailoring, shoemaking, poultry and retail. This set the unbroken 7% disability inclusion benchmark sustained through 2026.
