News report · 2024 · 18 pages · PDF

Xtra-Inter-Intra Religious Dialogue News 2024

JISRA (Joint Initiative for Strategic Religious Action) · Western Uganda FBO Network · “Because religious influencers are everywhere…”

A record of WUFBON's work under JISRA: the background and editorial statement, our focus and commitments, and the year's key activities, with women, youth, people with disabilities and queer individuals at the centre.

  • Facilitating intra-religious dialogues
  • Documenting legal referrals for SGBV victims
  • Social media campaigns on Freedom of Religion or Belief (FoRB)
  • Sensitization dialogues with refugees
  • Inter-religious edutainment with a cultural troupe and the Interfaith Brass Band
  • Inclusion of traditional religious identities
  • Engagement with district security committees
  • Dissemination of the Albinism Study Report
  • Success stories from religious, cultural, youth and queer actors
  • Innovation, technology and research in the JISRA initiative
