# wufbon website
The official website of the Western Uganda Faith-Based Organisations Network (WUFBON). Under the banner "Temba Emeeza — Climb the Table: One Movement!", WUFBON mobilises 800 religious leaders and 146 member faith-based organisations across 10 district interfaith hubs in Kyenjojo, Kyegegwa and the Albertine Graben. The site presents the network's six programs, its verified track record since 2013, publications and learning materials, and ways for donors and partners to invest, including direct giving to the Caren McNelly McCormack Women and Children Health Facility
