Temba Emeeza. Climb the Table: One Movement!

Rooted in Faith. Driven by Justice. Built for Generation-Lasting Self-Reliance.

We are transforming ordinary citizens from passive recipients of development decisions into active owners of their future. Uniting 800 religious leader subscribers and 146 member Faith-Based Organisations across 10 district interfaith hubs and 3 sub-regional hubs in Kyenjojo, Kyegegwa and the wider Albertine Graben, WUFBON builds locally owned ecosystems of specialized healthcare, inclusive green schools, clean-energy retail micro-franchises, wildlife defense and riverine green businesses, engineered to achieve 100% financial self-reliance by 2031/2032.

Who we are

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

An accountable, courageous, self-reliant interfaith movement

An accountable, courageous, and self-reliant interfaith grassroots movement across Western Uganda where climate change adaptation, sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR), bodily autonomy, clinical excellence, economic justice, wildlife protection, and ecological stewardship are realized for all people.

Our Mission

Rooted in faith, feminist principles and civic solidarity

WUFBON mobilizes 800 religious leader subscribers and 146 Faith-Based Organisations across the Albertine Graben to pioneer climate-health integration, advance sub-national health policy decentralization, secure grassroots worker power, provide stigma-free financial inclusion, protect vital water basins and wildlife corridors, and deliver quality foundational education through bold social enterprise and non-partisan civic advocacy.

For institutional donors

Why donors invest in WUFBON

Four structural pillars make every partnership build lasting, locally owned capacity.

1

The Cross-Subsidizing Business Model

We do not run charity conduits; we build income-generating assets. The private clinical wing at the Caren McNelly McCormack Women and Children Health Facility, commercial advertising slots across our 25-station Interfaith Media Network, and eco-hospitality fees at Nsinde Eco-Beach generate permanent local revenue to cover 100% of core operational overheads and cross-subsidize free care forever.

2

The Last-Mile Clean Energy Retail Engine

Rejecting market-distorting handouts, WUFBON trains and supplies interfaith youth chapters, women's Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs), and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) to operate as certified retail micro-entrepreneurs selling tier-1 solar home systems and clean cookstoves.

3

Last-Mile Predictive Data (The Interfaith 52 Model)

Trained interfaith health and youth organizers use mobile digital applications to map childhood immunization gaps, log real-time HIV/AIDS diagnostics, track mental health and nutritional burdens, prevent unsafe abortions, and secure medicine supply lines at the last mile.

4

Trusted Interfaith Monolithic Infrastructure

Anchored inside 800 religious leader subscribers, 146 FBOs, trade associations and workers' unions, we rapidly de-escalate land, mining and climate-induced conflicts through the Alternatives to Violence Project (AVP) while preserving safe civic space for marginalized groups.
