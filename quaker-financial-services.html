Program 4 · The Pink & Queer Economy Engine

Quaker Financial Services

Stigma-free capital for marginalized entrepreneurs. Traditional banking systematically excludes people because of identity-based discrimination or physical vulnerability.

How it works

A revolving Pink Economy Capital Fund

Operating as an autonomous, non-discriminatory micro-investment platform, Quaker Financial Services manages a revolving Pink Economy Capital Fund.

Supported by a 2025 partnership grant from Stone Run Friends Meeting and grounded in Quaker testimonies of equality, peace and human dignity, The Pink Fund provides safe, stigma-free, zero-collateral revolving micro-loans, savings mechanisms and financial literacy mentorship for queer and marginalized entrepreneurs in rural Western Uganda.

Confidential, peer-led savings circles help participants launch and sustain micro-enterprises such as tailoring, agriculture, retail trade and artisan crafts, while fostering a protected space for mutual aid, psychosocial solidarity and economic self-reliance.

Direct seed capital

Accessible, stigma-free micro-investment seed loans to sexual minorities, social minorities and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) across Uganda.

Enterprise & cooperative skilling

Training grassroots entrepreneurs in financial literacy, transparent cooperative bookkeeping and regional market linkage.

Institutional bias reform

Workplace inclusion training modules for commercial banking networks to dismantle structural discrimination across the financial sector.

Co-invest in community-owned assets

Your contribution is catalytic seed capital for revenue-generating social enterprises that sustain themselves.
